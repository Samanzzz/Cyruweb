02 — How it works

Capital, counsel and craft.

A venture capital firm invests in young companies. A consulting firm advises them. CyruTech does both — and builds alongside founders until they’re ready to scale.

Discover

We meet founders early, pressure-test the idea and the market, and decide together whether there’s a real fit.

Invest

We back the company with capital and take a stake in its success — our incentives are aligned with yours.

Advise & build

Our consultants and studio teams work hands-on across strategy, product, go-to-market and data.

Scale

Traction compounds. We prepare the company for its next raise and open doors to investors.

04 — The program

Ninety days. Built to compound.

We work with companies at every stage — from a conceptual spark to an established product — in a focused three-month sprint where urgency meets opportunity.

  1. Month one

    Refine

    Sharpen the product, the customer and the story. Cut what doesn’t matter; double down on what does.

  2. Month two

    Grow

    Put the product in front of users. Build the channels, partnerships and feedback loops that expand your base.

  3. Month three

    Raise

    Turn traction into investment readiness — and leave positioned for sustainable growth, not just the next step.

Seasoned mentors

Operators and founders who have been where you are — and are invested in getting you further.

Strategic partners

Warm introductions to the customers, channels and partners that unlock your next stage.

Investor access

Preparation and introductions so you walk into every raise with traction and a sharp story.
