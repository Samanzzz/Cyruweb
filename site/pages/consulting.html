Questions

Is the first conversation really free?

Yes. Your first consultation is free and comes with no commitment. It’s 30 minutes to understand where you are and whether we can help.

Do we need to be raising money to work with you?

No. Plenty of our consulting work is with founders who aren’t raising yet and established businesses that simply want an expert eye.

Do you only advise, or do you also build?

Both. We can hand you a plan, or deliver it with you — product through SullDog, marketing through AdverCal and data through Cloud Impala.

How is pricing set?

Every engagement is scoped to your needs, so pricing is tailored to the work. We’ll talk it through after the discovery call, before anything starts.

Where do you work?

We’re based in La Jolla, California, and meet in person or online — whatever suits you.
