About CyruTech

Pioneering innovations, shaping tomorrow.

CyruTech Capital is a venture capital and consulting firm based in La Jolla, San Diego. We catalyze the growth of innovative technology startups — and turn visionary founders into tomorrow’s industry leaders.

Our story
Our goal

We don’t just invest money. We invest in potential.

Our overarching goal is to catalyze the growth and success of innovative technology startups. We engage with companies at every stage — from conceptual sparks to those with established products — and propel them to new heights of development and market penetration.

Over a focused three-month acceleration period, each company refines its product, significantly expands its user base and becomes markedly more attractive to investors.

Founders gain access to seasoned mentors, strategic partners and potential investors, all unified in their commitment to the startup’s success. That network, combined with tailored guidance, helps founders unlock levels of performance that often surpass their own expectations.

By the end of their time with CyruTech Capital, our startups are positioned not just for the immediate next step, but for sustainable growth and leadership in the tech industry.
